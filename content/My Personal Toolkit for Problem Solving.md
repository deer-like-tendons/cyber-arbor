# Prerequisite:
1. Clearly define the problem that you want to solve
2. Ensure the problem you've stated is not a proxy for a bigger problem (the bigger the problem you can attempt to solve, the better)

### Physiological Tools:
- Neurotransmitters:
	- Less entertainment and more boredom results in greater dopamine sensitivity
		- Result: Improved concentration, motivation, and pleasure derived from effort
	- NDSR/Naps to replenish dopamine reserves and clear mental RAM:
		- Result: Improved C, M, PDfE
	- Mindful use of stimulants (coffee/music) can reduce "wasted" dopamine on tasks that don't require it
		- Result: Improved C, M, PDfE for tasks of interest

### Deliberate Fantasy:
- LARP-ing
	- Imagine oneself to be a fictional character
		- Result: Assume the demeanor, mindset, and/or resolve of
	- Make sure other's know you're always cooking something up
		- Result: Instant boost in confidence of whatever you're doing
	- View the struggle/hardship as an opportunity to learn and grow (Bumstead 6th Olympia Loss (that never materialized 🐐))
- Thought Experiments:
	- Ask yourself "How would you solve this problem if you had 20x agency"
		- Result: Better solutions just appear
	- Ask yourself "What would you do if you had to succeed at this?"
	- Ask yourself "What would you do if you assumed full ownership of the situation?"
	- Ask yourself "What's the most prudent thing for me to do in this moment?"
	- Ask yourself "What would be helpful in this moment?"
	- Ask yourself "What if I had to deal with this problem for ever?" or "Could I be happy if this never resolved?"
		- Result: Shifting away from trying to solve an issue can reduce aversion/suffering

### Social Support:
- Reach out to others for advice
	- Result: Talking about problems feels good, and others can provide useful insight. Additionally certain peers can supply accountability in the future.
- Journal about the problem:
	- Result: Writing about problems can make them less unwieldy
- Talk to an LLM
	- Result: Kinda like talking to a friend and journaling, but always available and more insightful than some friends
- Find/Consume content about the topic:
	- Result: Hearing others discuss the problem or tangentially related topics can provide insight.

### Meditation/Insight/Conjurer's Tricks:
- Don't turn away and contract from, but turn towards and open up to:
	- Result: Tanha reduction
- Direct attention towards an inert object (breath, sight, etc)
	- Result: Thwarts unwanted thoughts and results in sedation
- Meditate Regularly
	- Result: Improve concentration, clarity of experience, and equanimity

### Misc:
- Intellectually ascertain a better approach
	- Result: No limits; Can result in anywhere from an incremental solve to a total solve.
- Queue up overnight ECS Task (Sleep on it w/ intent of letting subconscious stew on it)
	- Result: Some chance for clarity to emerge the next morning, plus an immediately reduced burden
- Recall the luck of my great fortune
	- Result: Reduction in petty sufferings
- Recall my dreams and aspirations
- Imagine how I would look back at this problem while on my deathbed
- Perform a physical intervention to change my surroundings
	- Move an object to a different location (either for emphasis or obscurity)
	- Place a reminder where needed
- Recognize that discomfort is the catalyst for improvements:
	- Result: A more positive, empowered perspective is found
