### Constitution:
- **Body**:
	- My fitness (strength, mobility, endurance, agility, resilience, or work capacity) improves by an appreciable amount year over year
	- I live an active life style in which I can partake in a variety of physical activities while still having requisite energy to attend to my other physical and mental goals.
	- I minimize activities that result in shortening my lifespan and health-span
- **Mind**:
	- My ability to concentrate improves by an appreciable amount year over year.
		- Concentration is skill that is required to accomplish anything from work to hobbies. I feel comfortable pursuing improved concentration for the mere sake of concentration ability, as I believe it will pay dividends later.
- **Mental Health**:
	- My leisure and work time are both executed in a way that is in accordance w/ how I feel in the moment and how I will likely retrospectively feel.
		- Too often I look back at my days and am unhappy with how I spent my time.
	- I do not depend on consumerism or luxury items to foster a positive state of mind; I could be happy living like a college student.

### Craft:
- I'm paid to do intellectually stimulating work that I enjoy, and I work roughly as much as I'd prefer to.
	- This requires an exceptionally rare and valuable skill, or luck to encounter a perfect employment opportunity. I don't believe I currently possess a skill rare and valuable enough to ensure I can find this opportunity, so I must spend time improving my hardware (attention)
- I'm dedicated to showing appreciable year over year improvement in at least one form of high quality leisure (sport, creating, writing, etc)

### Community:
- I have a relationship with my wife that I would be proud to display to my children
	- We treat each other with respect
	- We place egos aside and communicate honestly and genuinely
- I connect with friends or family on a regular basis and in an enjoyable way

### Contemplation:
- I curiously explore appearances in my consciousness through meditation
	- All phenomena are inherently mysterious and can always be examined as such
- I watch for opportunities in which I attempt to avoid an emotion so that I can learn more about myself.
- I, on occasion, opt for unusual decisions so long as they don't welcome permanent damage to myself or community
	- I will not elaborate further

### Celebration:
- I make time for live music with friends and family
- I partake in non-destructive usage of psychedelics with friends and family
