## Step One: Become a Capable Human Being

### Discipline:
Establish 3 keystone habits
- Body -- 
	- Exercise
	- Diet
- Mind -- Making your mind sharper
	- Probably should involve reading
- Heart --  Involves other people 
	- Check-in with a different friend or family member every day
	- Setup more regular calls w/ friends
	- Always make time to reflect w/ Mersina

### Control / Organization
Exercise control over your time to ensure intentional and focused energy expenditure.:
- Cal Newport's multi-scale planning methodology
- David Allen style "Full Capture" every obligation is written down in a trusted system (nothing kept in the head). This includes a plan for when the obligations will be done

### Craft:
- Learn to do something really well (Quality Focused)
- Build appreciation for quality in craft
- Starting w/ a hobby is okay
	- "Learning how to do things really well teaches you how to do things really well"
### Simplification:
- Slash obligations that don't support goals
- Simplify technological life
	- Phone foyer lifestyle
	- Uninstall apps
	- Delete Accounts
## Step Two: Cultivate Depth
### Values:
- Reflect on your values, defining a personal code that guides your decisions and actions.
- Establish rituals that connect you consistently to these values, fostering a deeper sense of purpose.

### Service:
- Use your newfound capabilities to serve others in meaningful ways.
- Embrace leadership roles in family, community, or larger societal contexts, making a non-trivial sacrifice on behalf of others.

### Transformation:
- Make big value changes towards your goal life style that aligns w/ your values
	- Changing Jobs
	- Moving to the country side

### Legacy:
- What impact do you want to leave on the world
- (Does not need to be addressed on the first pass through the stack)
