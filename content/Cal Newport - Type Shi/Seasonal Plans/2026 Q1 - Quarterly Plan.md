## Gist:
As this is my first formal attempt to move towards the deep life, my first quarter goals focus on modest improvements to things like distraction management, schedule adherence, and time allocation. 
## Key Stone Habits:
1. No Tech After 10PM
	-  This should support all of the following:
		- More, better sleep (wonderful for all aspects of life)
		- Opportunity to read
		- Opportunity to meditate
		- More quality time w/ wife
	- Less tech and more of the other stuff should marginally sharpen my mind
2. Phone is never brought to my work desk
	- Less distraction at work
	- More comfortable being disconnected from my phone for long periods
3. Block off at least one 30 minute "Deep Work" block on weekdays
	- More deep work is good
	- Beginner steps at forecasting my work schedule
	- Beginner steps at protecting my time from my colleagues
4. Start daily workout <30 minutes after starting my lunch
	- I already workout daily, but have recently been starting that later and later. The time from ~12PM until I start is mostly wasted on low quality leisure. Starting my workout kicks off a chain of Exercise -> Shower -> Eat -> Optionally Nap -> Get back to work
	- More practice adhering to a schedule

## Other Areas of Focus:
- Finding interesting work
	- Given this is an introductory quarter, I'm okay simply exploring what work I may want to pick up. This work should be:
		- Inherently interesting to me
		- Utilize my skills or innate talents
		- Not overly daunting
- Learning about Cal Newport's and adjacent system
	- I think my KeyStone habits and Described Desired Life are pretty solid, but I don't feel I have fleshed out all aspects of Cal's view of the deep life.
	- I want to fully understand:
		- [x] Multi-Scale planning
		- [x] Time blocking
